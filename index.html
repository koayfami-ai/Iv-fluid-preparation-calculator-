Formula & Reference

Dextrose & Saline

General Mixing Formula

Σ(C × V) = Cf × Vf

Where:
C = Concentration
V = Volume
Cf = Final Concentration
Vf = Final Volume

Albumin Dilution

Dilution Formula

C₁V₁ = C₂V₂

Albumin Required
V₁ = (C₂ × V₂) ÷ C₁

Diluent
= V₂ − V₁

Sodium Bicarbonate

Required mmol

Target mmol/L × Volume(L)

Bicarbonate Volume

Required mmol ÷ Stock mmol/mL

Diluent

Final Volume − NaHCO₃ Volume
